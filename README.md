### 🍔 Food Delivery App – Project Description

This **Food Delivery App** is a simple, colorful, and user-friendly mobile application designed to make ordering food **quick, easy, and convenient**. The app provides a smooth experience from browsing food items to successfully placing an order.

### 🌟 Key Features

* **🏠 Attractive Home Page** – A clean welcome screen with a food-themed logo, tagline, and easy navigation.
* **🍕 Food Menu** – Displays different food items with **food images, names, prices, and Add+ buttons**.
* **➕ Add to Order** – Users can easily select their favourite food items and add them to their order.
* **💰 Price Display** – Each food item clearly shows its price, making it easy for users to choose within their budget.
* **🛒 Order Management** – Selected food items can be added to the order before checkout.
* **📍 Delivery Address** – Users can enter their address for food delivery.
* **📱 Phone Number** – A phone number field is provided so the delivery person can contact the customer.
* **💵 Cash on Delivery** – Provides a simple payment option through cash on delivery.
* **🧾 Total Bill** – Shows the total amount of the order before placing it.
* **✅ Order Confirmation** – After checkout, the app displays an **“Order Placed Successfully”** message.
* **🚴 Track Order** – Users can track their order after it has been placed.
* **🔙 Back to Home** – Users can easily return to the home page after completing an order.
* **⚡ Fast Delivery** – The app highlights quick delivery with a **15-minute delivery** feature.

### 🎨 Colourful & Visual Design

The app uses a **bright and attractive colour palette** to create an energetic food-ordering experience:

* 🟡 **Yellow/Orange** – Used for food-related highlights, prices, and important elements.
* 🔴 **Red** – Used for attention-grabbing buttons and branding.
* 🟢 **Green** – Can be used for success messages, fresh-food categories, and positive actions.
* ⚪ **White** – Keeps the interface clean and prevents the colourful elements from looking overwhelming.
* 🖼️ **Food Images** – Large food images make the menu more attractive and help users quickly identify dishes.
